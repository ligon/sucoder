* Important choices

- Keep =CODEX_HOME= and authentication in place; set absolute =sqlite_home= on the independently owned server.
- Keep state outside the clone and Git, with private shared recovery generations and local active databases.
- Installed Codex =0.162.1= has six observed SQLite databases, including separate thread history; test every required store and maintenance lock on Savio.
- A warm per-database backup is not automatically an application-consistent bundle.
Sealed status requires profile-verified graceful shutdown; crash candidates do not acquire that status merely because writers stopped.
- Durable offer/claim/revoke records, lineage exclusivity and serialized parent pins prevent duplicate history owners and retention races.
- Renewal keeps the old allocation alive after draining its service, launches a specifically authorized replacement, verifies real Codex readiness, then cancels the old allocation.
The current confined adoption path can otherwise rediscover the old job even after session YAML is cleared (=sucoder/mirror.py:3058=).
- Preserve both old and pending-new attempt records; a surviving tmux shell is not replacement readiness.
- Concurrent restored forks are deferred until upstream thread/external-file isolation is established.
