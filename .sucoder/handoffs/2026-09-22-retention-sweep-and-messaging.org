* Constraints learned

- The classifier denies =gh pr merge= and any write under =~/mirrors/=
  on the cluster.  Do not retry; say so.
- =detect_changes= after an insertion names the line-shifted neighbours
  of the insertion point as changed; check the hunk headers before
  believing its risk level.
- Never put a closing keyword next to =#N= in a commit message.  Write
  "issue 14".  The rule is in =AGENTS.md= now.

** Issue 33, closed by PR 40

The worktree-clean prompt no longer has a discard answer; the stash is
named (=sucoder: stashed by user@host before push, <time>=) and the
prepare script announces such stashes; untracked files are listed first
and counted; an answer not on the menu re-prompts.  Verified live
through =agents-clone= against a throwaway mirror under
=~/sucoder-test-33= (removed afterwards).  =push= goes through =sync=
and never reaches the prompt; =agents-clone= is the light command that
does.  A first push to a mirror that does not exist refuses as
unverified and needs =--allow-unverified-mirror=; =agents-clone=
initialises it cleanly.
