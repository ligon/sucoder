* Mission

A =sucoder -T carleton-htc collaborate= sat at "waiting for RUNNING..."
printing one =squeue= progress line per 15s poll and saying nothing
about why; after ten minutes it would have given up with "still
PENDING".  The likely cause was a BRC maintenance reservation that the
target's =--time=12-00:00:00= could not clear.  Previous handoff:
[[file:handoffs/2026-09-22-retention-sweep-and-messaging.org]].

* What landed

Branch =fix/pending-poll-reason=, two commits on local =main=
(df2f7b4).  The =coder= account cannot push (the =github= remote has no
credentials here; =ligon= is =no_push=), so the push and the PR are the
human's.

1. 3070daf :: =_poll_confined_node= (sucoder/mirror.py:2801) asks
   =squeue= for =%T|%N|%r|%S=, logs the pending reason and estimated
   start once and again on change, heartbeats about every 2 min, prints
   a progress line for the first probe only (the 52379fa rule), and puts
   the reason in the timeout error.  =_pending_hint=
   (sucoder/mirror.py:278) names the =--time= request and the two
   ways to shorten it when the reason is =ReqNodeNotAvail= or a QOS
   wall-duration limit.
2. 5ff2791 :: =sucoder --time LIMIT= on the main callback
   (sucoder/cli.py:1747), validated against SLURM's time grammar
   (=_SLURM_TIME_RE=, sucoder/cli.py:1385), refused without a SLURM
   target, applied in =_build_executor= (sucoder/cli.py:684) to the
   target's =SlurmConfig= before anything is submitted.  README
   documents it beside the slurm target keys.

* Constraints learned

- =git worktree add= fails here: =.mcp.json= carries a git-crypt smudge
  filter and =coder= holds no key.  Compare against =main= with
  =git show main:<path>= instead.
- =detect_changes= on pure insertions names every line-shifted
  neighbour as "touched" and rates the diff critical; read the hunk
  headers (=git diff -U0 | grep '^@@'=) before believing it.
