* Constraints learned

- The classifier denies =gh pr merge= and any write under =~/mirrors/=
  on the cluster.  Do not retry; say so.
- =detect_changes= after an insertion names the line-shifted neighbours
  of the insertion point as changed; check the hunk headers before
  believing its risk level.
- Never put a closing keyword next to =#N= in a commit message.  Write
  "issue 14".  The rule is in =AGENTS.md= now.
