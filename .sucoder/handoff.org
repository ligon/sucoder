* Findings that change the design

- All six databases follow =sqlite_home=; =-c= beats =CODEX_SQLITE_HOME= beats =config.toml=; a relative value resolves against the cwd; a missing directory is created with =0777= masked by umask; an unwritable one fails the launch with exit 1 and no fallback.
- Thread-writer locks, =installation_id=, plugin and arg0 locks, the =sessions/= rollout JSONL and the TUI's =history.jsonl= stay in =CODEX_HOME=; the rollout is written and re-read, so it is an external transcript dependency the generation must address.
- The app-server and the TUI own their database descriptors with no daemon socket; =remote-control --no-daemon= opens its stores under the override, creates =/tmp/codex-daemon-<uid>/<hash>= and a startup lock in =CODEX_HOME/app-server-control/=, then needs credentials.
- A zero exit after stdin EOF leaves all data in WAL; "sealed" must check WAL state or use the backup API.
- =agent_message_board_1.sqlite= is a flag-gated seventh store; no maintenance lock was observed.
- The real Codex home sits on Lustre because NFS =$HOME= is mounted =local_lock=none= (the human confirmed this); 1656 =F_SETLK= calls in a six-second idle turn is the contention mechanism.
