* Uniqueness does not identify the household
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: household-grain
:END:

The canonical schema calls =i= the household, =t= the wave, and =j= the item; the shocks index is =(t, i, Shock)=.
Those names alone do not choose a raw household identifier.
The [[file:../pilot.org::#tz-notes-july][Tanzania survey-design note]] explains how the =2008-15/= four-round construction back-casts descendant panel lines into earlier waves.
=UPHI= and =r_hhid= therefore describe different entities: a descendant panel household constituted at Round 4 and the household in a particular round.
UPHI can make the index unique while counting descendant lines.
With =r_hhid=, repeated household-round records become unique only after guarded de-replication.
The round scope matters, and household-level replication should not be generalized to every individual-level module.
