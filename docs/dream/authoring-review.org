* Findings

** R1 HIGH -- Preserve the existing repair capabilities and route findings explicitly
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: repair-contract
:END:

The [[file:authoring-plan.org::#sequence][initial patch vocabulary]] (=docs/dream/authoring-plan.org:72=) permits replacing a citation and adding a reference, while prohibiting prose and task changes.
The existing [[file:../../sucoder/dream_wiki.py][repair loop]] (=sucoder/dream_wiki.py:320=) serves both mechanical failures and independent-review findings.
The narrow vocabulary therefore cannot replace that loop's general repair contract.
This combines overlapping findings from the contract and effectiveness reviewers.

Concrete regressions include restoring the omitted =--session-id <uuid>= qualification, producing supported prose after a reviewer rejects an unchanged decision, and withdrawing a proposed unsupported task completion.
Those paths are exercised by [[file:../../tests/test_dream_wiki.py][the missing-flag repair]] (=tests/test_dream_wiki.py:120=), [[file:../../tests/test_dream_curate.py][rejected-no-change correction]] (=tests/test_dream_curate.py:83=) and [[file:../../tests/test_dream_editor.py][failed-authority correction]] (=tests/test_dream_editor.py:258=).
Even within reference-only mechanics, neither proposed operation can consolidate duplicate references to one page/target while retaining their different necessary citations.
That was the actual repaired defect in both [[file:runs/sucoder-e3.org][E3]] (=docs/dream/runs/sucoder-e3.org:76=) and [[file:runs/sucoder-e5.org][E5]] (=docs/dream/runs/sucoder-e5.org:72=), and duplicate references remain rejected by [[file:../../sucoder/dream_wiki.py][the validator]] (=sucoder/dream_wiki.py:228=).

Required revision: distinguish reference-patch repair from semantic/disposition repair and retain an explicitly bounded route for the latter.
Complete the reference operation vocabulary or route unsupported reference changes through the retained general repair path.
The coordinator must choose the route using trusted findings and the frozen policy; a model cannot obtain wider edit authority by requesting a fallback.
Both routes share the existing allowances and require whole-candidate re-review.
An intentionally unsupported finding needs a durable terminal disposition before another futile model call.

Acceptance: retain all three existing convergence cases, plus duplicate-reference consolidation that preserves both source citations.
Prove reference patches cannot modify prose/tasks, fallback retains human/source constraints, and route changes do not grant another automatic repair or bypass review.

** R2 HIGH -- A resumed phase must restore context authority and accounting
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: checkpoint-context
:END:

The [[file:authoring-plan.org::#sequence][checkpoint inventory]] (=docs/dream/authoring-plan.org:79=) names parent proofs and source/window identities, but does not explicitly require restoration of accumulated lookup state and its resource/authority semantics.
A new [[file:../../sucoder/dream_context.py][Session]] (=sucoder/dream_context.py:33=) initializes request history, byte/search counters and delivered windows.
The current session also distinguishes a search snippet from a fully delivered page that may be edited.

A phase implementation could verify the old draft and then construct a fresh session: the next repair would receive a fresh lookup allowance, or a previously delivered supporting window would disappear from validation.
Restoring all inventory pages as editable instead would grant authority never established by full-page delivery.
This is a requirement for the new phase boundary, not a finding that current recovery already leaks data.

Required revision: authenticate and reconstruct the existing [[file:../../sucoder/dream_context.py][Session.payload]] (=sucoder/dream_context.py:203=) and lookup transcript, including used/request allowances, exact windows, full-page deliveries and archive context.
Derive counters and edit authority from the verified responses rather than creating a second independent counter store.
Freeze the intended allowance scope explicitly; a continuation must not gain a new context grant merely by acquiring a new stage ID.

Acceptance: exhaust lookup/byte allowances before checkpointing and verify they stay exhausted on continuation.
Retain authority for a verified full-page delivery, reject snippet-only editing, and replay accumulated context without fresh retrieval or native startup.

** R3 HIGH -- Define a logical repair round separately from native attempts
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: repair-accounting
:END:

The [[file:authoring-plan.org::#sequence][recovery promise]] (=docs/dream/authoring-plan.org:84=) allows another repair after a failed repair, while the preceding rule forbids replenishing repair allowances.
The term failed repair currently covers different states.
The wallet records [[file:../../sucoder/dream_budget.py][native completion]] (=sucoder/dream_budget.py:335=) before proposal/mechanical/independent acceptance; E6's final repair was completed at that layer and still failed citation coverage.
The [[file:../../sucoder/dream_wiki.py][max_repairs loop]] (=sucoder/dream_wiki.py:320=) is not a persistent logical-round state machine.

With one allowed repair, a completed but mechanically rejected revision has used the existing revision opportunity.
Allowing another revision under a fresh stage ID could evade that limit; forbidding every failed native attempt would instead defeat the proposed transport-failure recovery.
The distinction cannot be inferred from the wallet's completed/failed label alone.

Required revision: specify a durable logical-round identity, consumption point and outcome, separately from each charged native attempt.
Define the permitted explicit retry transitions for denied startup, unknown/provider failure, a completed body, mechanical rejection and semantic revision.
Any retry remains separately reserved and charged; unknown work is not silently repeated.
If the semantic repair allowance is spent, stop unless a separately defined, recorded authorization permits additional work.

Acceptance: contrast transport failure with completed-but-rejected repair, restart each state and attempt to fork stage IDs.
Verify no fork obtains an extra revision opportunity, all native reservations remain charged and the terminal disposition is stable under replay.

** R4 MEDIUM -- Protect reviewer calls as well as reviewer time
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: review-capacity
:END:

This is a pre-pilot requirement, not a blocker to milestone 1.
The [[file:authoring-plan.org::#sequence][review-only reserve proposal]] (=docs/dream/authoring-plan.org:99=) is expressed primarily in seconds.
The existing [[file:../../sucoder/dream_budget.py][closeout call pool]] (=sucoder/dream_budget.py:228=) is shared, and [[file:../../sucoder/dream_author.py][reviewer lookups]] (=sucoder/dream_author.py:74=) require another native response before the final judgment.
Repair/lookups can therefore exhaust native calls while protected wall time still remains.

Required revision: define protected call and elapsed capacity, including the final verdict and bounded reviewer lookup behavior.
Specify what happens when further context cannot fit; insufficient evidence must not turn into acceptance or an implicit allowance extension.
Reuse the shared wallet and explicitly version any new policy.

Acceptance: leave ample time but few calls, and prove repair cannot spend the protected reviewer calls.
Exercise a reviewer lookup near exhaustion and verify the final decision capacity or a durable bounded stop is preserved.

** R5 MEDIUM -- Separate valid editorial disposition from pilot route coverage
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: pilot-coverage
:END:

This is a rollout gate; reviewed no-change remains a valid dreaming outcome.
The [[file:authoring-plan.org::#gates][fixed-evidence pilot]] (=docs/dream/authoring-plan.org:155=) and [[file:authoring-plan.org::#gates][pass condition]] (=docs/dream/authoring-plan.org:158=) currently allow a no-change result to stand in for evidence that the new native patch path works.
Existing [[file:../../tests/test_dream_curate.py][no-change execution]] (=tests/test_dream_curate.py:65=) uses only author and reviewer calls.
Both pilots could legitimately return no-change while no native model ever produces a patch or exercises its materialization.

Required revision: record feature-exercise evidence separately from editorial disposition.
Require an actual native patch response to materialize into a complete candidate and reach independent review before marking that route verified.
Keep deterministic crash/checkpoint recovery tests offline if their boundary is explicit.
If the approved pilot does not exercise the route, report it unverified; do not force novelty, alter old native artifacts or extend approved calls to manufacture coverage.

Acceptance: a no-change run passes its editorial disposition but leaves patch coverage unverified.
A separately planned, properly bound native patch case supplies the missing route evidence under its approved limits.

** R6 MEDIUM, advisory -- Freeze a small semantic quality floor before reducing cost
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: quality-floor
:END:

This need not block milestone 1.
The [[file:authoring-plan.org::#sequence][effort/length experiment]] (=docs/dream/authoring-plan.org:98=) proposes cheaper, shorter work, but [[file:authoring-plan.org::#gates][quality evaluation]] (=docs/dream/authoring-plan.org:161=) does not yet name the critical qualifications or reader questions that must survive.
A fluent shorter page could obtain reviewer acceptance while losing a distinction a later reader needs, such as local merge versus observed GitHub publication.

Required refinement before effectiveness claims: freeze a few source-grounded qualifications and held-out reader questions before construction, with the judging rubric outside the builder context.
Reuse the [[file:workplan.org][existing fresh-reader/source-fallback approach]] (=docs/dream/workplan.org:174=) and [[file:workplan.org][reader evaluation work]] (=docs/dream/workplan.org:266=), rather than adding a large evaluation system.
Compare cost only after those checks pass, preserving the plan's warning against generalizing from one run.

* Constraints already handled

The plan preserves source admission, bounded lookup, immutable raw replies, independent full-candidate review and sole publication authority.
It gives Talk no blanket citation exemption, keeps unreviewed drafts distinct from accepted progress, and retains historical contract dispatch and E6's incomplete disposition.
Dynamic evidence handles remain request data, while changed native policy/schema/control combinations require certification.
No API/account switch or automatic unknown-work retry is proposed.
The six-call/twenty-minute allowance is a reasonable proposed smoke-test ceiling, not observed reviewer latency or authorization to run it.

* Disposition

The original review left R1-R3 open for the full release, R4-R5 open for pilot sign-off and R6 as an evaluation refinement.
The follow-up closes those design findings at the reviewed revision; implementation and live effectiveness remain unverified by design review.
