* 3. Definitions and conventions

A job id is 32 lowercase hexadecimal characters; an explicit path keeps its existing meaning.
Implicit missing config uses derived defaults; an explicit missing path and malformed existing YAML fail.
Dream startup never prompts or modifies configuration, even when both harness CLIs are installed.
Unknown provider outcomes remain charged and cannot be silently retried.
Saved jobs and assignments retain their existing frozen identities and validated-result checks.
Explicit =--retry-seconds= is bounded to 1-86400 seconds and must exceed the frozen closeout reserve.
Retry records form a checked append-only sequence keyed by attempt; reopening a recorded window keeps its original deadlines.
Original budget descriptors and reservations bind the original policy, while new advice records the active window's deadline and old advice remains replayable.
