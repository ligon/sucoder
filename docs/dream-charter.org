* 0. Question, restated

Design =sucoder dream= to turn accumulated project evidence into a useful, checked-in Org companion wiki for human readers and future agent sessions.
Dreaming should revise understanding across sessions: explain relationships and decisions, correct mistakes, preserve uncertainty, and keep relevant knowledge easy to find.
The companion supplements the project's maintained documentation and explicit human decisions.

This workshop delivers a design and a bounded pilot plan.
Runtime implementation and operational rollout require later work.
The candidate projects are SuCoder and LSMS_Library; their different documentation practices should test whether the design generalizes.

** Decisions already agreed in conversation

- Keep the companion wiki in the project's git repository, provisionally under =.sucoder/wiki/=.
- Write readable topic pages and an index in Org, with evidence and useful cross-links.
- Attach editorial discussion beneath the relevant article heading as a =Talk= subheading tagged =editorial=.
- Express editorial work with real Org TODO headings and the applicable workflow, including completion records.
- Archive completed editorial subtrees that have lost immediate relevance into another checked-in file.
  Preserve a concise active rationale and a link when a decision still constrains editing.
- Keep archives available for retrieval while excluding them from the ordinary context reading set.
- Keep raw transcripts and temporary extraction products outside the tracked wiki.
- Preserve human contributions and distinguish proposals, observations, inferences, and adopted decisions.

The [[#wiki-task-workflow][wiki task workflow]] is declared and exercised in this charter.
The human approved the charter and this initial workflow on 2026-11-08; design review may propose subsequent changes with reasons.

** Premises to challenge

A wiki earns its maintenance cost only if it helps readers answer questions or avoid mistakes that existing documentation and source search already address.
Page counts, link counts, and apparent completeness are inadequate success measures.

Existing notes may already perform much of the proposed function.
LSMS_Library deliberately keeps country-specific reasoning beside country code in =_/CONTENTS.org=; a second inventory of those facts would risk disagreement and drift.
The design must explain when the companion links to existing knowledge, when it synthesizes across sources, and when it proposes a correction to maintained documentation.

A transcript is evidence of recorded statements and actions, with limitations.
Repeated summaries, fork histories, or assistant assertions cannot independently establish that an action succeeded or a scientific question was resolved.

** Operational conclusions from the research

:END:

The conclusions below constrain the design and its [[#definition-of-done][acceptance criteria]].
The cited findings motivate them; the SuCoder adaptations are proposals for this workshop, not results established by Anthropic's evaluations.

*** Correct existing memory and synthesize across sources
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: repair-memory
:END:

The [[https://www.anthropic.com/research/components-of-agent-memory][essay's correction example]] checks a mistaken note against its transcript; another section reports cross-session gap repair.
Require a dream to inspect relevant existing pages alongside new evidence, revise unsupported claims, and seek relationships that individual sessions missed.
Turn supported explanations into reusable rules; leave competing hypotheses and missing verification as explicit Talk tasks.
Support a bounded maintenance pass over existing material even when no new transcript has arrived.
The worked lifecycle must begin with a plausible wrong note and show its correction, evidence trail, and expected effect on a subsequent reader's answer.

*** Make the wiki a route to evidence
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: retrieve-evidence
:END:

The [[https://www.anthropic.com/research/components-of-agent-memory][essay's retrieval experiments]] found a benefit from combining wiki and source search; synthesis tasks benefited more than some direct lookups.
Retain source retrieval as part of the reader contract, with links that identify what can be checked and where.
For LSMS, explain connections among country notes while directing country-specific questions back to the relevant =CONTENTS.org= sections.
The pilot must compare documentation plus source search, wiki plus the same source search, and a wiki-only diagnostic condition.

*** Budget what readers load and test that they use it
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: reader-context
:END:

The [[https://www.anthropic.com/research/components-of-agent-memory][essay's injected-context discussion]] favors a small top layer; its steering discussion shows model-dependent memory use.
Specify an explicit startup reading allowance, a short orientation, topic selection, and archive access rather than loading the entire index or wiki without a bound.
Supply an example reader instruction that locates memory, requests relevant evidence, and makes reading behavior observable in a later pilot.
Treat relocation into an archive as a context saving only when the normal reader actually excludes that file.

*** Set the purpose of memory and preserve essential distinctions
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: purpose-and-fidelity
:END:

The [[https://www.anthropic.com/research/components-of-agent-memory][essay's salience experiments]] caution against restrictive rules such as recording only failures; another experiment improves results by tailoring instructions to the task.
Write a concrete dream brief for the intended human and agent uses, allowing useful plans, successful procedures, explanations, and tested rules as well as mistakes.
Name the details whose loss would change an answer, such as a command flag, survey grain, wave scope, or unresolved maintainer decision.
When evaluation reveals a recurring omission, propose a revision to that brief and re-evaluate it; automatic changes to project instructions remain out of scope.

*** Separate analysis from consolidation and account for coverage
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: evidence-workflow
:END:

The [[https://platform.claude.com/cookbook/managed-agents-cma-dreaming-with-a-dynamic-workflow][dreaming cookbook]] analyzes inputs before a final organizer and checks completed work against execution records.
Specify concrete extraction, verification, consolidation, and publication phases, their inputs and outputs, and which role may write each artifact.
Preserve the ordering and distinction of user statements, assistant claims, tool calls, and results when preparing transcript evidence.
For an input too large to process in one read, define complete chunk coverage or an explicit incomplete outcome.
The run report must reconcile admitted, examined, skipped, and failed evidence independently of the dreamer's prose summary.
Coverage records establish what evidence was supplied to a role, not whether the model understood it correctly.

*** Prevent consolidation from erasing another contribution
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: preserve-contributions
:END:

The [[https://github.com/anthropics/claude-quickstarts/blob/main/managed-agents/knowledge-wiki/README.md#cost-and-tier-expectations][wiki quickstart reports lost lines]] from concurrent writes to shared pages.
Use disjoint extraction artifacts followed by one publisher as the proposed default; justify any alternative that permits shared writers.
Carry source references, temporal qualifications, disagreements, and human edits through consolidation and verify their survival before accepting the result.
Create a scenario where two inputs contribute different necessary facts to one topic and check that both remain available after consolidation and indexing.

*** Make repeated dreaming selective and bounded
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: selective-maintenance
:END:

The [[https://www.anthropic.com/research/components-of-agent-memory][essay's closing discussion]] identifies staleness and repeated checking costs as open problems; its examples include wiki growth beyond the source corpus.
Define triggers for revisiting a topic: changed evidence, a reader failure, an unresolved Talk task, a broken reference, or a fact likely to have changed.
Require a stopping rule and separate allowances for evidence reading, consolidation, and reader context.
The pilot plan must distinguish a first build, an ordinary incremental update, and a targeted repair pass, accounting for cumulative maintenance and reading costs.
No assumption that nightly rebuilding or another pass always improves the result is part of the charter.

*** Evaluate the construction method as well as its prose
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: evaluate-construction
:END:

The [[https://github.com/anthropics/claude-quickstarts/blob/main/managed-agents/knowledge-wiki/README.md][wiki quickstart]] measures construction and answering costs; its prompts retain sources, conflicts, and superseded claims.
Freeze the evidence subset, dream and reader instructions, model and harness identities, and output revision for each later comparison.
Include direct-lookup controls, cross-source synthesis questions, a seeded wrong-memory case, and a time- or scope-sensitive caveat.
Revise the construction brief using a separate development set; retain held-out questions for comparison so the dream is not supplied its evaluation answers.
Do not transfer published model rankings, prices, or break-even claims to these repositories without measurement.

** Wiki task workflow
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: wiki-task-workflow
:END:

Use the file-local keyword declaration at the top of this charter as the initial workflow for wiki editorial tasks.
Each wiki source must declare the settings directly or include them from a checked-in Org setup file, so interpretation does not depend on a contributor's personal Emacs configuration.
Task states describe work on knowledge; claim confidence and decision authority still belong in the article's evidence and attribution.
Preserve the native task states of existing source documents when reading them.

- =TODO= :: Captured work that is not yet selected and ready to start.
- =NEXT= :: Scoped, actionable work with no unresolved prerequisite; an owner can take it next.
- =ACTIVE= :: Work currently underway, with its responsible contributor named in the entry.
- =WAITING= :: Work held on a specific dependency, missing evidence, or external decision.
  Record the blocker, link it where possible, and state what permits work to resume.
- =REVIEW= :: A proposed result is ready for a named review or decision.
  Record the reviewer, what is being reviewed, and what acceptance requires.
- =DONE= :: The task's outcome is checked and incorporated, including any explicitly required review.
  Preserve the evidence or decision and a completion timestamp.
- =MOOT= :: Work intentionally dropped, rejected, or superseded.
  Record why; link a replacement when one exists.
  This is a terminal disposition, not a claim of successful completion.

The bar in the keyword declaration separates open work from the two terminal states, DONE and MOOT.
Use REVIEW for the proposed result awaiting a decision and WAITING for work that depends on that decision.
Tasks may take the transitions their work requires; they need not visit every state in sequence.
For example, ACTIVE may move to WAITING and return to NEXT, or move to REVIEW and return to ACTIVE when revision is requested.
A completed item may be reopened when evidence changes; record the reason and preserve its earlier resolution.
Assignment is metadata rather than an additional state: delegation changes the responsible contributor, while the work still has one of these states.

Record state changes in LOGBOOK drawers, using timestamped notes for entry to WAITING, REVIEW, and MOOT.
The declaration requests timestamps for the other states and on exit from WAITING or REVIEW; =logdone= records completion timestamps and =logdrawer= keeps state records out of the main prose.
An agent performing a transition must record the same note and timestamp information that Org would request interactively.

Terminal state makes an item eligible for archive review when it has lost immediate relevance.
An archive candidate must have no open descendant tasks, and any decision still needed by active readers must retain a concise rationale and working link on the active page.
The [[#acceptance-editorial-lifecycle][worked editorial lifecycle]] must demonstrate these checks.
